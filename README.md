# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis — Excel

## Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Excel to perform end-to-end sales, profitability, customer, product, regional, and shipping analysis on the Superstore dataset.

The project transforms raw transactional data into a cleaned dataset, analytical summaries, an interactive-style dashboard, and business-focused insights. The analysis focuses on identifying revenue drivers, profitability patterns, loss-making product areas, regional performance, customer segments, and shipping behaviour.

The project demonstrates practical Excel skills required for a Data Analyst role, including **data cleaning, aggregation, KPI analysis, PivotTable-based analysis, dashboard development, trend analysis, and business insight generation**.

## Business Objective

The objective is to answer key business questions such as:

- How are overall sales and profitability performing?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue and profit?
- Which sub-categories contribute to profit or loss?
- Which regions perform best and which require improvement?
- Which customer segments generate the highest sales?
- How do sales change across years?
- Which shipping modes are most frequently used?
- Where are potential profitability and operational improvement opportunities?

## Dataset

The analysis uses the **Superstore transactional dataset**, containing **9,994 records** and 21 business-related fields.

## Analytical Approach

The analysis was structured around several business dimensions.

### Category Performance

Sales and profit were evaluated across:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the three major categories.

### Sales Trend Analysis

Sales were analysed across the available years to identify overall growth patterns.

Annual sales increased from approximately **$484K in 2014 to $733K in 2017**, indicating substantial growth over the analysis period.

### Customer Segment Analysis

Sales were compared across:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

The Consumer segment generated the highest sales at approximately **$1.16M**.

### Regional Profitability

Profit was analysed across the four major regions:

- Central
- East
- South
- West

The **West region** generated the highest profit at approximately **$108.4K**, while the **Central region** generated approximately **$39.7K**.

### Sub-Category Profitability

Profitability was analysed at the sub-category level to identify strong and weak product areas.

**Copiers** were among the strongest contributors to profit, while categories such as **Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies** showed negative profitability in the analysis.

This helps identify product areas that require pricing, discount, or cost review.

### Shipping Analysis

Order volume was analysed by shipping mode.

Standard Class accounted for the largest number of orders, with approximately **5,968 orders**, while Same Day accounted for approximately **543 orders**.

This provides a basis for evaluating the relationship between shipping preferences, service levels, and operational costs.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Technology is the strongest major category

Technology generated approximately **$836K in sales** and **$145K in profit**, making it the strongest major category in both revenue and profitability.

**Business implication:** Technology represents a significant contributor to overall business performance and can be investigated further to identify the products and customer groups driving its success.

### 2. Furniture has relatively weak profitability

Furniture generated approximately **$742K in sales** but only around **$18.5K in profit**.

**Business implication:** The relatively low profit compared with sales suggests that pricing, discounting, product costs, or product-level profitability should be investigated.

### 3. Sales show strong long-term growth

Annual sales increased from approximately **$484K in 2014 to $733K in 2017**.

**Business implication:** The business experienced strong overall revenue growth, but the underlying drivers of this growth should be examined by product, customer segment, and region.

### 4. West is the strongest region by profit

The West region generated approximately **$108.4K in profit**, while Central generated approximately **$39.7K**.

**Business implication:** The factors contributing to West's stronger profitability can provide useful benchmarks for improving performance in weaker regions.

### 5. Some sub-categories generate losses

Several sub-categories recorded negative profit, including **Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies**.

**Business implication:** Loss-making products should be reviewed for discount levels, pricing strategy, procurement costs, and demand before continuing or expanding those product lines.

### 6. Standard Class dominates shipping volume

Standard Class accounted for approximately **5,968 orders**, significantly more than the other shipping modes.

**Business implication:** The high dependence on Standard Class provides an opportunity to evaluate whether the shipping mix effectively balances delivery expectations, customer requirements, and operational cost.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Review pricing, discounts, and costs associated with low-margin and loss-making sub-categories.
- Investigate the drivers behind Technology's strong profitability and identify opportunities to expand successful product lines.
- Analyse the factors contributing to the West region's stronger profitability and apply relevant practices to weaker regions.
- Evaluate customer-segment purchasing behaviour to identify opportunities for targeted sales strategies.
- Monitor the high-volume Standard Class shipping channel and evaluate its cost-to-service balance.
- Separate revenue growth from profitability growth when evaluating business performance, particularly in categories with high sales but relatively low profit.
